## Quick Win Tips (rotate per industry)

- **Dental/Medical:** "Add `alt` attributes to your team photos — right now they're invisible to screen readers."
- **E-Commerce:** "Set `aria-label='Close'` on your modal dismiss buttons — keyboard users are currently trapped."
- **SaaS:** "Add `role='alert'` to your form error messages — screen readers won't announce them otherwise."
- **General:** "Check your heading hierarchy — skipping from H1 to H3 confuses screen reader navigation."

## Notes

- **Timing:** Space follow-ups 5–7 business days apart. Max 2 follow-ups, then stop.
- **Tone:** Keep it casual and helpful, never pushy. The goal is to be the person who genuinely offered to help.
- **Personalization matters:** Always reference something specific from the audit (the pain_point). Generic = deleted.
- **Track in spreadsheet:** Log send date, response (if any), and next action date per prospect.
